# The Card: how to work with the almanac (25 Oct 2026)

Douglas, the afternoon after a weekend of losing slips, asked for rules for
how to use the almanac and changes to match: bet the day before or in the
morning and leave it, no checking in half way, one fixture per group,
groups that play over the same hours (overnight, daytime, evening), all
sports, and every group returning 2.0 or 3.0+. Stake size is not his worry.

## The playbook (on the page)

1. Place once: Overnight by 21:00 the night before; Daytime and Evening
   once they lock at 07:00.
2. Leave it: no checking mid-game, no cash out, no topping up.
3. Only the card. Anything else goes through the slip checker; Avoid means out.
4. Best price: the bookmaker shown beside each pick.
5. Review weekly, on Mondays, from the scoreboard; change nothing mid-week.

## The build (`groupings.py`, `build_card`)

- `CARD_WINDOWS`: Overnight 00-09, Daytime 09-17, Evening 17-24, UK time of
  kick-off (`uk_slot`). A leg with no kick-off time is left out.
- `CARD_KINDS`: Card 2x (2.0-3.0, floor 30%), Card 3x (3.0-4.6, floor 18%).
  3-4 legs (`CARD_LEGS`), max two from one competition, no fixture in two
  groups the same day.
- `card_fault`: priced, 1.15-1.90 (`CARD_ODDS`), bookmakers' fair 60%+
  (`CARD_FAIR_MIN`), model 40%+ and no more than 5 points above the market,
  not `listOff`. Football legs are already clean (`legs()` drops Celtic's Law,
  unrated, thin and the rest).
- Chosen for the highest combined chance at the bookmakers' own numbers
  (`best_card`), the pool ordered by chance given up per unit of price.
- `lock_time`: Overnight 21:00 UK the day before, Daytime and Evening 07:00
  UK. Before: `recheck_card` keeps a group if every leg still passes, else it
  is rebuilt (logged). After: frozen, status `locked` (or `underway`). A
  window with nothing at its lock can take a `late` group from games not yet
  started, locked as built.
- `archive_locked` adds each locked group to `groupings-archive/<date>.json`
  once. `grade()` adds `card` and `cardByWindow` tallies; Card kinds are the
  only `current` kinds, the rest are listed as retired.

## Prices for every sport

Football: API-Football (paid, all of it). NFL, NBA, MLB, NHL, college
sports, WNBA: ESPN scoreboards, but ESPN only carries odds near the game
(25 Oct: NFL 12 of 12, NBA 4 of 19, college football 3 of 3). Tennis, rugby:
The Odds API free plan; credits are not the limit (4 of 500 used in
October), coverage is: on 25 Oct it listed one of six tennis events and no
active rugby union league. A paid Odds API plan ($30 a month for 20,000
credits) buys credits, not coverage. Better candidates, Douglas's call:
API-Sports' own rugby, basketball, hockey and baseball APIs (the same
account as API-Football, each with an odds endpoint), and a second
odds source for tennis. Until then the card is mostly football at midday
and US sport overnight.

## Open

- College football and tennis stay out (`listOff`) until their live record
  holds; the card will then take them automatically.
- Whether an unlocked group should be allowed to change at all after the
  evening before. Today it can, until 07:00; Douglas may prefer the
  evening-before card for every window.

## Free price sources, probed and wired in (25 Oct 2026, afternoon)

`tools/odds_probe.py` in CI (`odds-probe.yml`, report on branch
`odds-probe-report`): API-Sports' rugby, basketball, hockey, baseball and
american-football products answer on the API-Football key, free plan, 100
calls a day each; odds by game only (`/odds?date=` is rejected). Wired in
as `price_api_sports` (`AS_HOST`, `AS_DAY` 85). PinnWire (Pinnacle, 13 sports,
tennis included) needs a free personal key: the demo key's shared 50 a day
is gone early. Wired in as `price_pinnwire`, inactive until the
`PINNWIRE_KEY` secret exists; one `sport_id=all` prematch call per build.
Terms on showing Pinnacle prices on a public page were not stated; check
before relying on it. ESPN's rugby scoreboards rejected a date range (400);
not pursued.
