# Scale: Daily List winners with a second condition (15 Nov 2026)

## What Douglas asked for

14 Nov 2026: "it's not really about scaling the stake so much. It's actually
more about scaling the odds and even reducing the stake, reducing the losses
... another tab on this website called Scale." A five-leg winner and both
teams to score accumulator had been quoted at about 300 times the winners-only
price. Then, on the confidence bar: "I don't want to come down from confidence
banding. I still want to make sure we're picking results that are definitely
going to win. That's still the most important thing ... threefold, fourfold,
fivefold ... The important thing is to track and have visibility on that
tracking ... similar to the daily list, where it's like the dots." On the
first mock-up: "I wasn't able to find the Hungary NB third league or the
Greece Gamma Ethniki league ... those aren't available on William Hill" and
"my worry is we won't have enough football picks per day". He approved:
football at William Hill and tennis straight sets first, with tracking; the
other sports' margin bets after a price check.

## What was built

`scale.py` (standard library, fetches nothing), run by `deploy.yml` after
`markets.py`. A Scale pick is a Daily List pick (`daylist.pinned`, so the
copy published before its start) plus one more thing that must happen:

| Sport | Scale bet | Chance shown | Price |
|---|---|---|---|
| Football | the pick wins and both teams score | the model's own number (`markets.py` `wbtts.p`) | William Hill's (`wbtts.h`); a list pick Hill does not price is counted as left off |
| Tennis | the pick wins in straight sets | the record's rate for picks in its confidence band (`tennis_rates`; a band under 30 picks takes the pooled 75%+ rate), shown as "N of M picks like it" | none yet |

The list's bar is untouched: nothing below it can be a Scale pick.

- **Archive**: `scale-archive/<UK day>.json`, bot-written. A pick is replaced
  until it starts and kept as it stood from then on. A started pick not yet
  on file is still added, because every field comes from copies archived
  before its start (the predictions archives and `predictions-markets/`).
- **Groups**: per UK day, a threefold, fourfold and fivefold (`KINDS`) of the
  picks still to start that are most likely to land their Scale bet, nested.
  Replaced until the first leg starts, then frozen. No group under three
  picks; nothing is padded.
- **Grading**: football from `record-graded.json` (through
  `markets._result_for`), tennis from `tennis-record.json` (`straight_sets`
  reads the score in the row's `note`). States: `scale` (won and the Scale bet
  landed), `won` (won only), `lost`, `void` (a retirement or walkover:
  bookmakers void the bet). A group is lost as soon as one leg misses, won
  when every standing leg landed, and its return is counted only when every
  standing leg had a William Hill price.
- **Live v before**: `LIVE_FROM` 2026-11-15. Days before it are filled once
  from the pre-start archives (tennis only: no price archive existed to say
  which football picks Hill priced), marked `replay`, and tallied apart
  (`before`). `record.bySport` is the tab's own record.
- **Output**: `scale.json` / `scale.js` (`window.__SCALE__`, gitignored,
  copied to `_site`): `days` (today and tomorrow), `glance` (14 days of
  dots), `record` (by sport, groups by kind, recent groups), `before`,
  `replay` (football list picks before the tab in leagues Hill prices now;
  the tennis bands), `leagues` (where Hill priced win and both score in the
  last 14 days, from the markets archive).

The tab (`renderScale` in `index.html`, third in the bar after Bets): the
record tiles, Scale at a glance (one line a day, a dot a pick: solid green
won and landed, ringed green won only, red lost, open to play, small grey
not priced at Hill), the day's list (winner bar, Scale bet and its chance,
Hill's price with the break-even price as "needs"), the groups, the leagues
Hill prices, and the scoreboard. "Price too short" marks a pick or group whose
Hill price is under the price its chance needs.

## What the numbers say so far

- Football Daily List picks in leagues Hill prices (before the tab, results
  only): 43 picks, 35 won (81%), 14 won with both scoring (33%).
- Tennis, every graded pick at 75%+: 243 picks, 213 won (88%), 160 in
  straight sets (66%); by band 75-80% 57 of 101, 80-85% 42 of 63, 85-90% 36
  of 49, 90%+ 25 of 30.
- 1 to 14 Nov tennis list picks as published: 35 of 42 won, 27 in straight
  sets.
- The first day (15 Nov): 11 football list picks, 2 priced at Hill (New
  Zealand 2.80 against a 29% chance, needs 3.39; Finland 3.50 against 22%,
  needs 4.50), 9 left off, no group. Both prices are under what the model's
  number needs.

## What to be straight about

- **Volume.** Hill prices win and both score in about 36 leagues a fortnight
  on the feed; most Daily List football picks are in leagues it does not
  price. Football alone is about two picks a day and none on many days. The
  men's tennis tour ends with the ATP Finals this week and restarts in
  January. Days without a group will be common until other sports are added.
- **Value.** On the first 66 priced fixtures the bookmakers' fair chance for
  win and both score was 20.6% against the model's 19.2%: the model has not
  shown an edge on this bet. The football markets gate (`markets.py`, 150
  priced picks) is the test of that. The Scale tab tracks; it does not claim
  the bets pay.
- **Tennis chance is a tally.** It is the record's own rate, so it moves as
  the record grows. Nothing is fitted (Rule 1).

## Not changed

No model, the Daily List, the Card, or any other tab. No bookmaker input
into a model: prices decide only which football picks are shown here.
