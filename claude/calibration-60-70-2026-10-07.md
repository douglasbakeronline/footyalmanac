# The 60-70% band: noise, not a miscalibration (7 Oct 2026)

Task: picks quoted 60-70% won 71% vs 66% expected over 96 games on the live
record. Is that real? Answered from history (the `tune.py` walk-forward
replay, live curve `a = 1.135, b = -0.30`), never `record.json` (Rule 1).

## Numbers (`python3 calibration_bands.py --football`)

| Set | n | quoted | landed | gap | 90% interval | P(gap <= 0) |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Fit season 2025-26 (in-sample for the curve) | 603 | 64.2% | 64.8% | +0.6% | -2.5 to +3.9 | 0.376 |
| Check season 2026-27 (out of sample) | 124 | 64.1% | 73.4% | +9.3% | +2.5 to +15.7 | 0.013 |
| Pooled | 727 | 64.2% | 66.3% | +2.1% | -0.8 to +5.1 | 0.119 |

## Reading

- On the 603 fixtures of the last full season the band is calibrated to
  within 0.6 points. The 2026-27 check season is the only place the gap shows
  (+9.3, one-sided p 0.013), and that is 124 games from the first two months.
- That p-value is one band of about eleven, two-sided in spirit. Allowing for
  the number of bands looked at (about 0.13) it is not distinguishable from
  noise. Neighbouring bands in the same season go the other way (55-60%:
  -3.6 points), which a real tilt of the curve would not do.
- The live record's 71 vs 66 over 96 games sits inside the same noise (one
  standard deviation at n = 96 is about 4.8 points), and the check-season
  replay and the live picks are largely the same games, so they are not two
  independent confirmations.

## Could the curve fix it?

`tune.py --fit --dry-run` searched the whole (a, b) grid on the fit season.
Best candidate `a = 1.155, b = -0.30`: check-season log loss 1.0267 -> 1.0268
(+0.0001, sd 0.0002), P(worse) 72%. Gates `notWorse` and `worthIt` FAIL, so
nothing was written. A tilt strong enough to lift 60-70% would hurt 55-60%
and 70%+ on the same fixtures. `calibration.json` is untouched.

## What changed

`calibration_bands.py` now prints a 60-70% span (n, quoted, landed, gap,
interval, bootstrap P(gap <= 0)) under each football table, so the question
can be re-asked when the 2026-27 season has more games. Advisory only.

## Reopen when

The check season holds 300+ games in this span and the gap is still above
about +4 points with the interval clear of zero; then run `tune.py --fit`.
