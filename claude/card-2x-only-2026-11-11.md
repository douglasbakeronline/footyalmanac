# The Card builds the 2x alone (11 Nov 2026, CARD_RULE 8)

Douglas, 11 Nov 2026, after the Card became the model's own picks (rule 7):
"Just do better on the groups."

## What was tested

Six shapes of the rule 7 Card, each one change from live, pre-registered in
`tools/card_replay.py` (`m7edge3`, `m7edge6`, `m7legs3`, `m7short`,
`m7only2x`, `m7notennis`) and in `tools/card_legs_history.py` (`cardM7`,
football-data, the model's legs). The football-data side ran in CI over five
seasons (`card-legs-history.yml` on `card/better-groups`, 2022-23 to 2026-27).

The 3x under rule 7 won 10 of 24, 2 of 11, 4 of 45 and 4 of 32: one positive
season in four. The 2x alone won 41-55% of its groups in three seasons of
four. Three picks a group (`m7legs3`) is the same as live; demanding a bigger
edge (3 or 6 points) made it worse, as the 29 Oct finding said (picks the
model rates above the market land at the market's rate).

## Decision

`CARD_BUILD = ("Card 2x",)`, `CARD_RULE` 8: the Card builds up to three 2x
groups a day (the replay's count), two units each. The 3x stays in
`CARD_KINDS` for ranges, the record and the page; `with3x` replays it beside
live. Under the 100-leg pool gate (36 legs) and level with live in 2023-24, so
not a formal pass; the build session took it on Douglas's instruction, with
the replay ahead in three full seasons of four and on both pool reads.

## Not changed

The leg rule (rule 7), stakes per group, the filler (now 2x only too), the
Daily List, any model. Roll back: `CARD_BUILD = ("Card 2x", "Card 3x")`,
`CARD_RULE` 7.
