## The rule (`groupings.daily_group`)

Tiers in order: Daily List, reserve, football at 65%+ on the model, any
other sport at 65%+. Within a tier strongest first. Price 1.10+ with a real
bookmaker price, not started, one a fixture, up to 8 picks, stop at 2.0. No
pick reaches 2x: the strongest slip is published with `short`. Held as first
built until its first start, then frozen (the List card's path). Graded as
`cardDaily`.

## Review

At 50 settled slips: won v expected (z), return at the recorded price, and
the tiers' legs landed v model. If won sits 2 standard errors under expected,
change the rule.
