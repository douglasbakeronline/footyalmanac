# The Card's league limit under rule 8, and `m7wide` (11 Nov 2026, evening)

Build session, 21:17 London. Advisory: no rule changed.

## What was wrong with the funnel

Under rule 7/8 the Card's legs pass `groupings.model_fault`, which had the
core-league limit hard-coded. `card_fault` (rule 6) reads `CARD_CORE_ONLY`;
`model_fault` did not. So `tools/card_funnel.py` could count "league" faults
but had no relief for it, and `claude/card-funnel-rule7-2026-11-11.md` named
the value demand as the one condition costing groups. It is not the largest.

`model_fault` now reads `CARD_CORE_ONLY` exactly as `card_fault` does (wider
means `card_league`: API-Football leagues, cups and new leagues still out).
`CARD_CORE_ONLY` stays True, so the live Card is unchanged: the replay's
`live` row is identical before and after (pool from 30 Oct, `--recover`:
12 groups, 8 won, +69%).

## Decision

Nothing to act on. 84 legs is under the 100-leg gate and no read has p(worse)
at or under 0.30; the first-price and closing reads lean against. It more than
doubles volume, so it is the variant to watch for an empty Card. The nightly
review prints it beside the others from tonight; the 30 Nov review reads it on
the legs from 10 Nov (`--since 2026-11-10`), which carry their first real price.
