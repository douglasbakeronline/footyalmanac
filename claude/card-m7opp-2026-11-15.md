# The opposition's price on the model's picks: m7opp9 and m7opp5 (15 Nov 2026)

## Why

Rule 6's opposition condition (the other side priced 9+, `CARD_OPP_MIN`) is
the one Card condition that held in every football-data season: 511
under-market legs over 2022-23 to 2026-27 landed 83.8% v 76.2% quoted, and
legs with the opposition at 4-6 lost in all five
(`claude/card-seasons-2026-11-09.md`). CLAUDE.md's decisions say "Opposition
9+ (rule 6) stays".

Since rule 7 (11 Nov) the Card is chosen by `model_fault`, and `model_fault`
never read the opposition's price. The condition went with the switch
without a decision or a replay. The first rule 11 group (14 Nov, a two-day
2x at 2.36) carried VVV-Venlo with the opposition at 4.83, and VVV lost.

## Pre-registered (written before any result was read)

- `groupings.CARD_MODEL_OPP_MIN`, empty live, read by `model_fault` for
  football picks only (a two-outcome sport's opposition price is the
  complement of the pick's). A pick with no opposition price fails it.
- `m7opp9`: the rule 11 Card with the opposition at 9+.
- `m7opp5`: the same at 5.0 (4/1), the Edge flag's floor since 11 Nov.
- Both run on the pool nightly (`tools/card_replay.py`) and on the five
  football-data seasons (`tools/card_legs_history.py` `cardM7`, paired by day
  against live in `cardM7Paired` / `cardM7Pooled`).
- The gate is CLAUDE.md's: ahead of live on both the football-data seasons
  and the pool, p(worse) at most 0.30 on 100+ legs. Until then the live rule
  stays as it is. Pool legs archived before 6 Nov carry no opposition price,
  so the pool read is also given from 6 Nov.

The reading: on the model's picks, refusing football picks whose opposition
is priced under 5.0 drops about one group in thirteen, and the ones it drops
are the losers more often than the price says. It is ahead of live in every
season, by a similar amount each time (+0.10 to +0.16 units a day), which is
the consistency Douglas asked for. Rule 6's 9+ floor is too strict for the
model's picks: the model rarely backs a side whose opponent is that long.

## Verdict at 15 Nov: not yet

The gate (CLAUDE.md, the delegated decisions): ahead on the football-data
seasons and on the pool, p(worse) at most 0.30 on 100+ legs. Football-data
passes pooled (450 legs, p 0.014; 2025-26 alone is 0.32). The pool is ahead
(p 0.07) but on 33 legs. So the live rule stays until the pool side reaches
100 legs, about a month at the current rate, or the 30 Nov review, whichever
reads it first. The nightly review carries both sides.

## What would change the rule

`m7opp5` (or `m7opp9`) ahead of live on the pooled football-data seasons
and on the pool, p(worse) 0.30 or under on 100+ legs: the next session sets
`CARD_MODEL_OPP_MIN` to that floor and bumps `CARD_RULE` to 12 with the
numbers, without asking (the delegated-decisions rule). Not before.
