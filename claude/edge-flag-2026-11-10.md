# The green edge flag (10 Nov 2026)

Douglas, 10 Nov 2026: "I need you to create a green flag on fixtures that
meet the optimal edge and opportunity", after the Daily List accumulator
investigation (`claude/list-accas-2026-11-10.md`) showed the list's picks
land as quoted but carry the full bookmaker margin.

## The rule (`groupings.edge_flag`, `EDGE_RULE` 1)

A football fixture gets the green **Edge** flag when all of these hold:

- its league is one of football-data.co.uk's 20 divisions (`edge_leagues()`,
  `odds.DIVS`): the leagues the five-season replay priced. Core leagues
  outside them (women's, the Americas, Russia, Norway) were never tested
  on price, so they get no flag;
- it is clean for the Daily List (not Celtic's Law, not unrated, `why` in
  `CLEAN_WHY`);
- the model's pick is a win (not a draw) and is also the bookmakers'
  favourite;
- the pick's average price is 1.15 to 1.90 (`CARD_ODDS`) and the bookmakers'
  fair chance is 60%+ (`CARD_FAIR_MIN`);
- the opposition is priced 9+ (`CARD_OPP_MIN["football"]`);
- the model sits no more than 5 points above the bookmakers
  (`MULT_ABOVE_BOOK`): picks rated above the market land at the market's rate.

It is the Card's football leg without the under-market band, because
near-market picks did as well on the replay (`claude/card-seasons-2026-11-09.md`).
It reuses the Card's constants, so a Card rule change moves the flag with it;
bump `EDGE_RULE` when the flag's own conditions change.

## The evidence

- Five seasons, football-data prices: favourites whose opposition was 9+,
  511 under-market legs, 83.8% landed v 76.2% quoted, +4% at the average
  price, +7% at the best (on quote only in 2022-23).
- 2025-26, all 60%+ favourites at 1.15-1.90 with the opposition 9+: 232
  legs, 84.1% v 76.0%, +3.3% average, +5.8% best.
- 2026-27 so far (check season): 52 legs, 80.8% v 76.8%, -1.7% average,
  +1.1% best. The edge is small and needs the best price.
- The site's own archive since 11 Sep, the flag applied to what was
  published (read only, nothing fitted): 43 distinct fixtures, 34 graded,
  27 landed against 25.8 quoted, -2.1% at the average price archived.

So it is a small, real edge on price over seasons, not a guarantee, and a
rare one: about 43 fixtures in two months, a handful a week, most at the
weekend. That scarcity is the "opportunity": the flag is meant to stand out.

## On the page

A green **Edge** chip under the confidence on any football row that
qualifies, on the Daily List and the full board alike (`edgeFlag` in
`index.html`, `--edge` #1c6b3a, white text 6.5:1), with a title giving the
price, the best price and the opposition's price. The row's detail opens
with a green panel (1px border, the standing decision) giving the evidence in
one paragraph. Checked at 1280 and 400 wide on a preview built from the
8 Nov archive.

## Kept for grading

`build.py` writes `edge` into `predictions/<date>.json`, so the flag's own
record can be graded from the archive once it has enough fixtures. Nothing
grades it yet; the obvious next step is a line in the nightly Card review
(landed v the bookmakers' chance, return at average and best price).

## Not done

No flag for tennis, the NBA or any other sport: no price condition has been
tested for them. No change to the model, the Daily List's order or the Card.

## Its own record (added later on 10 Nov)

`score.py` now grades the flag (`edge_of`, `edge_record`, `record.json`
`edge`): a row archived with an `edge` key is **live** (the flag as published,
from 10 Nov; `None` there stays unflagged); an older row is **replayed**, the
rule applied to the prices archived with it before kick-off. Both are graded
at the archived average and best price. The record summary carries it
(`build.SUMMARY_KEYS`), the chip's title and the detail panel quote it, and
the nightly Card review prints it. At 10 Nov: 34 graded, 27 landed against
25.8 quoted (79.4% v 75.9%), -2.1% at the average price, +0.5% at the best,
12 Sep to 8 Nov; live 0 so far. Tests: `tests/test_edge_record.py`.
