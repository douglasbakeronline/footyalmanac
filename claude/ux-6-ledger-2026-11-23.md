# UX release 6: the ledger (23 Nov 2026)

Release 6 of the seven-release UX programme Douglas approved on 23 Nov 2026
("tracking sports and cards to remain consistent"). The review's point: one
tracker for every card, so the cards can be read the same way and compared.

## What was there

Five ways of drawing a card's record: `dailyTracker` (four stat tiles and a
grid of `.dx` day tiles), `slipTracker` and `edgeTracker` (the `.et` SVG profit
line and strip, with different range labels: "All / Live only / Last 14 days" v
"All time / Last 14 days"), the Card's `tbRecord` headline and `ogRecord` day
folds, Scale's graded-groups side panel in the scoreboard, and racing's
`rcRecordStrip` + `rcTracker` glance (borrowing Scale's dots) plus its own
profit SVG (`rcProfitSvg`) on the Record board. The List card had nothing.

## The component

All in `index.html`, after `edgeSection` (where `slipTracker` and `edgeTracker`
were):

- `ledger(id)` draws the card `LEDGER_CARDS[id]` describes. Anatomy, in order:
  the title and range control (`.seg.seg--sm`, `aria-pressed`), the headline
  sentence (`ledHead`: "<won> of <settled> won v <expected> expected ·
  <return> at the card's stakes · <n> to settle"), a sub line, the chart
  (`ledChart`: cumulative units, the profit line against the expected line, a
  faint zero line, ticks only at values the line reaches, the endpoint dot and
  its value, dates on the x axis, tap columns), the key, the strip (`ledStrip`:
  a tile a day with a coloured top rule, won / lost / void / placed, dashed for
  a day with nothing, dashed and plain for a replayed day), the day's detail and
  a note. `LED[id]` holds the range and the chosen day; one delegated click
  handler redraws (`ledDraw`) and keeps focus on the tapped control.
- `ledgerFold(id)`: on Bets every ledger but the Card's sits in a closed
  `details.led-fold` at the foot of its section; the summary carries the title
  and the headline, hidden while open (the open ledger shows its own, which
  follows the range).
- `LEDGER_CARDS`: `card`, `daily`, `list`, `slip`, `edge`, `scale`, `racing`.
  Each gives `ok()`, `ranges()`, `def()`, `since()`, `view(range)` (head, rows,
  lines) and `detail(day)`; `view0` is the tab the ledger lives on.
- Day rows: `ledGroupDays(match)` reads `groupings-record.json` days (each
  group's `stake`, `paid`, `p`, `odds`); these rows sum exactly to the record's
  tallies (checked for the Card, `cardLive`, `cardDaily`, `cardList`,
  `cardListLong`). `ledFill` adds the empty days between (Card, Daily 2x, List
  card).

## Where the numbers come from (nothing recounted)

| Card | Headline | Line and strip | Ranges |
|---|---|---|---|
| The Card | `cardLive` (live rule, the default) / `card` (all rules), as `tbRecord` | Card groups by day, two units a 2x | All rules · Rule 14 · Last 14 days |
| Daily 2x | `cardDaily` | its groups by day | All · Last 14 days |
| List card | `cardList` + `cardListLong` (one line each underneath) | List 2x and List long by day | All · Last 14 days |
| The slip | `slip.json` `record.recent` + `replayDays`, as `slipTracker` | the same | All · Live · Last 14 days |
| Edge picks | `record.json` `edge.days`, as `edgeTracker`; expected from `edge.all` / `edge.live` | best (solid) v average (dashed) price | All · Live · Last 14 days |
| Scale groups | `scale.js` `record.groups` (+ `before.groups` for All); returns weighted by the record's own `priced` / `estN` | `days[].groups` (Hill price, else the estimate, marked ≈) | All · Live (default) · Last 14 days |
| Racing tips | `racing.js` `record` (won, settled, profit, placed, beat SP) | `series` per tip; strip from `byDay` | All · Last 14 days |

A "Last 14 days" headline is the day rows summed (the records carry no
14-day tally). "Live" is the live rule for the Card (it has no replayed days;
its earlier rules play that part), and the days since going live for the slip,
Edge and Scale. Racing's feed keeps profit per tip, not per day, so its line is
drawn for All only and it has no expected line (no tip chance in the feed);
Edge's expected is left out of the Last 14 days range (not carried per day).
Scale's line starts on the first day `scale.js` holds (9 Nov); the replayed
groups before it are counted in the All headline, not drawn.

## Where each sits

- Bets: the Card's ledger open under the Card (`.tb-ledger`), its day taps
  opening that day's fold in Results, day by day (`ogRecord(true)` folds now
  carry `data-date`; a day older than the folds shows its groups in the
  ledger). The Daily 2x, the List card, the slip and Edge picks: a closed fold
  at the foot of each section. `tbRecord` keeps the rule line, the verdict, the
  sport table and More numbers; its won / lost / return headline is the ledger.
- Scale: at the foot of Scale groups; the scoreboard's side panel is gone.
- Racing: on Tips (foot) and Record (top, replacing the profit SVG).

## The Cards board

How it went's board picker gains **Cards** (`resGrid`, `cardsBoard`,
`#results?board=cards`): one row a card (the live record where it has one),
live since, settled, won v expected, return at its stakes, last 14 days and a
sparkline (`ledSpark`). Each row is a link, `#today?card=daily` (or
`#scale?card=scale`, `#racing?card=racing`): `navApply` reads `card` into
`LED_PENDING`, the view switch makes it `LED_FOCUS` and `ledGo` opens and goes to
the ledger; `navHash` writes `card` while the focus holds; any other tab turn
clears it. Back returns to the board.
