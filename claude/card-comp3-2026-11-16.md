# The two-a-competition cap on the model's-picks Card (16 Nov 2026)

## What prompted it

The funnel (`tools/card_funnel.py --since 2026-11-13`) on the pool at 16 Nov
03:17 UK: the UK day 16 Nov to 07:00 on 17 Nov has four NBA picks that pass
every Card condition (76ers 1.37, Rockets 1.60, Timberwolves 1.70, Warriors
1.70; the model 60-71%, all at or above the bookmakers' chance) and nothing
else, so no Card 2x: `groupings.mult_combo` allows two picks from one
competition (`COMP_MAX` 2), every NBA pick is "NBA", and a 2x needs three.
Over 13 to 19 Nov the funnel's "three legs a competition" row builds a 2x on
3 days of 7 (live 2) and 6 groups (live 4). Under rule 11 the NBA is the
Card's steadiest weekday source, so the cap now decides most weekday empties.

## What was on file

`comp3` (`COMP_MAX` 3) was pre-registered on 10 Nov
(`claude/card-funnel-2026-11-10.md`) and is replayed nightly on the pool on
the live Card. It was never in the football-data replay: the rule 7 list in
`tools/card_legs_history.py` (`cardM7`) left it out, so the 30 Nov review
would have had one side of the gate only.

## What changed

`comp3` is added to `cardM7` (and so to `cardM7Paired` and `cardM7Pooled`,
and the nightly review's gate lines). On football-data it means three picks
from one league in a group, the case where the cap may guard against shared
effects (weather, one afternoon's refereeing), so it is the harder test.
Nothing live changed: `COMP_MAX` stays 2, `CARD_RULE` 11.

## The gate

As every Card rule: beats live on the football-data seasons and the pool,
p(worse) at most 0.30 on 100+ legs. If it passes, the next session sets
`COMP_MAX = 3` with a `CARD_RULE` bump, without asking. A pass on the pool
alone with football-data behind would suggest a cap for football only and
none for the NBA; that would be a new variant, registered before its read.
