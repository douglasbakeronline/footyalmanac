## The three methods on the page before

| method | what it is | evidence | verdict |
|---|---|---|---|
| Edge picks | football favourites 1.15-1.90, bookmakers 60%+, opposition 9+, model no more than 5 points above | five football-data seasons: singles 388 of 459, +5.4% best price, +3.0% average, ahead in 4 of 5; doubles 170 of 252, +4.6% best; site 27 of 34, -2.1% average, +0.5% best (`claude/edge-accas-2026-11-10.md`) | the one leg with a tested edge over the price |
| The Card | 3-5 priced favourites in 2x / 3x groups, frozen before the first start | replay +4.4% over four seasons (+12.5% best, 2x positive every season); live 11 of 35 against 11.7 expected, -12.2%; tennis legs 22 of 40 v 28.3 expected, z -2.2 (`claude/card-seasons-2026-11-09.md`) | on trial; the 30 Nov review decides |
| The week ahead's model trebles | the day's top Daily List picks by model confidence, no price condition | accurate (list picks land as quoted) but the margin compounds: about -5% a leg, -32% at three legs on ten days of real prices (`claude/list-accas-2026-11-10.md`) | not profitable at the price |

## What the best case from each is

- From Edge: the leg. It is the only condition that held in every season.
- From the Card: the discipline. Built by the site, placed once before the
  first start, flat stakes, Hill's price, frozen and graded as published. And
  the double as the longest slip worth taking (the Card 2x and the Edge
  double both positive; trebles and longer swing with the season).
- From the week ahead: the forward view. What it should forecast is the
  plan's bets, not model trebles.

## What shipped (`index.html`, display only)

- **The plan** at the top of Bets (`signalsPanel`, `planRungs`): three rungs
  in order of proof, each with its stake and its evidence in one line.
  1. Edge singles, Tested, one unit each.
  2. Edge double, Tested, one unit, on a UK day with two or more open Edge
     picks: the two the bookmakers rate most likely (`sigList` builds it).
  3. The Card, On trial, at its own stakes (two units a 2x, one a 3x).
  Then four habits (best price, place once and late, flat stakes and leave
  it, nothing else) and **Today's bets**: every open bet in the next 24 hours,
  tested first then soonest, units and pounds, copy slip; later this week in
  a fold; the wider-rule filler in its own fold as not in the plan.
- **The week ahead** now counts the plan's bets per day (dots: green tested,
  gold on trial) with the day's list picks underneath, and the chosen day
  lists its plan bets with a link to the slips. The model treble and double
  sit in a fold, "Model picks for this day, not in the plan", with the
  reason.
- **The Card's** long rule and the play list are in a fold; the groups by day
  stay as they were. The Model card is in a fold, on paper.
- Bet rows are compact: the evidence is on the rung, not repeated per bet. Two
  columns on a desktop.

## What it did not change

No rule, stake, `CARD_*` constant, `CARD_RULE`, record or push. The Card is
still built and graded as before; the Edge double is not archived or graded
as a group (its legs are, through `edge_record`). If the double is to be bet
regularly, archive it at its first start as the Card is, so its record cannot
drift.

## Honest limits

- Edge picks are rare: about five a week, mostly at weekends, and only in
  football-data's leagues. Many days the plan is the Card alone, or nothing.
- The Edge edge needs the best price; at the average price singles made +3%
  and doubles broke even. The site's own 34 Edge picks are -2.1% at the
  average price.
- The Card rung is there because its replay is positive and its live record
  is within chance of break-even, not because it is proven. The 30 Nov
  review keeps it, changes it, or cuts it.
