# Where to trust the model (10 Nov 2026)

Douglas, 09:46: "add another insights development piece of work".

## What shipped

`trustmap.py` counts every graded pick at 55%+ in every sport by family and
confidence band, landed against the model's own number, with a z. `build.py`
adds the grid to the on-demand `record-data.js` as `trust` (first paint and
`record.json` unchanged). How it went, Daily List, shows it under "Is the
list honest?" as a table: green where a cell lands above its number past 90%,
red where it lands under, faint under 10 picks, and the clearly-off cells in
words above it.

Families: football core leagues (engine.LEAGUES, not cups or internationals),
wider leagues (af.*), cups and internationals; tennis by tour; each US sport;
rugby. Bands 55-65, 65-75, 75-85, 85-95, 95+. Strict win test.

## The read at 10 Nov

- Above its number: football core leagues at 55-65% (65% v 59%, 539) and
  65-75% (78% v 69%, 187), the mid-band finding of
  `claude/midband-calibration-2026-10-21.md`; college basketball at 55-65%
  (73% v 61%, 51).
- Under its number: football cups and internationals at 65-75% (56% v 69%,
  59), the cup shortfall in `claude/tuning-evidence.md`; college football at
  85-95% (78% v 89%, 32), why it is off the list.
- Everything else within chance, including tennis on both tours.

## What it is not

A tally of the scoreboard (Rule 1). It must never feed a fit: a band that
lands above its number is evidence for a calibration change only through the
replay gates, never from this table.
