# Which bookmaker to write the Card for (10 Nov 2026)

Oscar (Odds Analyst), HQ planner task. Advisory: `CARD_BOOK` stays "William Hill". No rule changed.

## Question

On legs that pass the Card's rule, which UK book prices best against the average and how often is it best? If one is clearly best, change `CARD_BOOK`; if the best price beats any single book by 2+ points, say so on the Bets page.

## Data

`tools/price_timing.py` over `groupings-archive/pool-*.json.gz` (30 Oct to 10 Nov build): 111 Card-rule legs with a first and last real price (one tennis leg dropped, last price after the start). Only football has UK books on these legs in volume. Live record used to locate the gap only; nothing fitted.

| book | legs priced | price v average | best on |
|---|---|---|---|
| Bet365 | 78 | +0.30% | 65% |
| William Hill | 86 | -0.04% | 62% |
| Betfair | 85 | -0.89% | 31% |
| BetVictor | 72 | -1.73% | 10% |

Paired, legs with both Hill and Bet365 (77): Bet365 longer by +0.43% on average, longer on 30, shorter on 24, equal on the rest; bootstrap one-sided p(Bet365 not better) 0.045. Best price v William Hill (84 legs with Hill and another book): +1.09% on average.

## Read

- Bet365 over Hill is 0.43 points on price on 77 legs from one fortnight, two-thirds of it ties at the top. That is under the 100-leg bar used for Card decisions and under any change worth a CARD_RULE or preference move. Douglas places with William Hill, and `CARD_BOOK` is a preference, not a filter, so a 0.4-point gap does not outweigh it.
- Best price beats Hill by 1.1 points, under the 2-point line, so nothing is added to the Bets page. (Per leg this compounds on a 3-5 leg group, about 4-5% on the combined price, but the page already shows every book's price in the "Prices by bookmaker" fold.)
- BetVictor and Betfair are clearly worse; neither is a candidate.

## Decision and re-check

No code change. Re-check when paired Hill v Bet365 reaches 100 legs: change the preference only if Bet365 is longer by 1 point or more with p(worse) 0.30 or under. The nightly Card review already prints the table (`price_timing.py` `books`).
