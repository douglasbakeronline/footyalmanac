# The Card's long legs: 60%+ picks priced 1.60 to 1.90 (18 Nov 2026)

Build session, 18 Nov 2026 03:17 London.

## Why look

Rule 13 (17 Nov) dropped the value demand, so the Card now takes a 60%+ pick
at any price in `CARD_ODDS` (1.15 to 1.90), including picks where the model
sits well above the bookmakers. 17 Nov's Card 2x carried one: Tonbridge
Angels at 1.81, the model 63%, the bookmakers about 50% fair.

At the last pre-start price the football 1.60-1.90 band reads the same (73
legs, 47.9% v 63.6%, z -2.78, -17.9%). Below 1.60 the model is on quote;
above it the picks land under the bookmakers' chance as well as the model's,
which is the old finding (CLAUDE.md: picks the model rates above the market
land at the market's rate or worse).

## Does it reach the Card?

Hardly. The Card's search picks the most likely 2x, and three legs at about
1.26 make 2.0, so long legs are rarely chosen.

- Pool replay (`tools/card_replay.py`, 11 Sep to 18 Nov, live is rule 13):
  live 44 groups, 18 won v 16.7 expected, return 0.940; `m7cap160` 41 groups,
  17 won v 15.8, 0.943; +0.03 units a day, p(worse) 0.41 on 123 legs. Under the
  gate. `m7short` (1.50) +0.06, p(worse) 0.43.
- The Daily 2x replayed on the ledger (strongest first, 1.10+, to 2x): 36
  slips, 4 legs over 1.60 in all; a 1.60 cap changes 4 days (+0.07 a day,
  p(worse) 0.24), a 1.50 cap 7 days and is worse (-0.06, 0.69). Too few days
  to act on.

## What was done

`m7cap160` (`CARD_ODDS` (1.15, 1.60)) registered in `tools/card_replay.py`
and in `tools/card_legs_history.py` `cardM7`, so the nightly review and the
Monday football-data read carry both sides of the gate. It was registered
after the ledger read but before any group replay; the football-data seasons
(2022-23 to 2026-27) are the out-of-sample side. If it passes the gate there
and on the pool (100+ legs, p(worse) 0.30 or less), it is a bookmaker
condition, so put it to Douglas with the numbers, as rule 13 was his.

## Not changed

`CARD_ODDS`, `CARD_RULE` (13), the Daily 2x, the record, the page.
