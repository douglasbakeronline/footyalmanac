# Two-legged ties and play-off series on the Card (25 Nov 2026)

Build session, 25 Nov 2026, 21:17 London. The Card's open group for 25 Nov
(Golden State Warriors, Memphis Grizzlies, Toluca) holds Toluca v Atlas, and
the archive also holds Atlas v Toluca on 29 Nov: a Liga MX Liguilla tie, rated
by the model as a league game. The Card keeps cups and internationals out
(`card_league`), but a league's own play-offs arrive under its league code.

## What is a tie leg

A fixture whose reverse (away v home) the same league's archive holds 1 to 8
days away (`tools/card_ties.py`, `TIE_DAYS`). Regular seasons do not repeat a
pairing that fast. Since 1 Nov the archive holds 180 such fixtures in leagues
the Card may take: Liga de Expansión (af.263) 26, Argentina's Federal A and
Primera B play-offs (af.134, af.129, af.132, af.131), Colombia's Primera B
(af.240), MLS 16 (the best-of-three first round), Venezuela, Liga MX 8,
Série B 6, the Southern League 4. In the core leagues: mx.1 8, us.1 16, ve.1
6, br.2 6, en.7ss 4, co.1 2. A fixture archived under two dates (a moved or
nominal round date) can make a near-miss; Série B's six should be checked
against its calendar before any rule reads this.

## The evidence so far

Too thin to act on. On the pool (settled, priced), football legs at 60%+ in a
Card league: 3 on a tie, 2 landed (model 66%); 243 not a tie, 76% v 68%.
Clear of the live rule: 2 tie legs, 1 landed. On the graded record (all
confidences, Card leagues, 21 Aug to 25 Nov): 123 tie legs landed 43.1% v
44.2% quoted; at 60%+ only 4 (2 landed). Core leagues at any confidence: 16,
landed 19% v 45% (z -2.1 on a sample this small, read as a reason to watch).

Why it may matter: the model cannot see the aggregate. A side protecting a
first-leg lead, or a favourite resting for the second leg, is the same
blindness Celtic's Law flags for cross-division cup ties.

## What shipped

The nightly Card review prints a "Two-legged ties and play-off series" table
(first and second legs apart, all Card-league legs and those clear of the live
rule) and names, in bold, any open Card group holding a tie leg still to
start. Watch only: no rule, page or record reads it.

## Pre-registered

When the pool holds 30 settled football legs on a tie clear of the live rule,
the next session adds a `notie` variant to `tools/card_replay.py` (a constant
`CARD_TIE_OUT`, off live, refusing tie legs) and reads it by the gate. Until
then no Card rule changes; `CARD_RULE` stays 14.
