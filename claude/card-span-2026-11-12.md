# Two-day Card groups (12 Nov 2026, CARD_SPAN)

Douglas, 12 Nov 2026, after 11 and 12 Nov built no group: "if you can't make
a card on 1 day - then look to the next day ahead to combine fixtures to be
able to create a card."

## What it does

`groupings.build_card`: when a day's window ends the full-rule pass with no
group, it tries one group from that day's unused qualifying picks plus the
next day's, under the same rule (the model's own picks, 2x, three to five,
two a competition at most, the context check), and keeps it only if it holds
at least one of the first day's picks. The group:

- belongs to the first day (archived and graded there), marked
  `span: [day, next day]`;
- is open until its first start like any group (so the first day's pick),
  re-checked on every build against both days' picks;
- takes its next-day picks off the next day's own Card: `main` passes the
  events it holds as `taken`, and an open next-day group that holds one is
  dropped and logged ("a pick is held by the day before's two-day group").

The page labels it "two days: Thu and Fri" with a line on when to place it.
The record keeps it in the Card tally and also apart as `cardSpan`.

## Evidence

Pool replay (`tools/card_replay.py --recover`, `span2`, 30 Oct to 14 Nov):

| | days with a group | groups | won | return |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| live | 15 | 28 | 15 | +33% |
| span2 | 17 | 30 | 16 | +31% |

Paired by day: -0.00 units a day, p(worse) 0.49. Two more days with a group,
one of the two extra groups won, the return unchanged within noise. Volume
without a cost on this sample; far under the 100-leg gate, so the owner's
call. It cannot be read on football-data seasons yet (the season replay
builds day by day); `span2` replays nightly.

## Limits

- The next-day picks are taken at an earlier price than they would be on
  their own day; prices lengthened from first to last on the archive
  (`claude/price-by-first-hour-2026-11-10.md`), so a little value is given up.
- Only picks within the context check's 30-hour reach get a verdict before
  the group is built; the rest are read on a later build and an "against"
  then drops the group.

## Roll back

`CARD_SPAN = False`.
