# UX release 5: the row (23 Nov 2026)

Release 5 of the seven-release UX programme Douglas approved on 23 Nov 2026
("Tracking sports and cards consistent"). The review's point: every pick is the
same row, wherever it appears, with the same star.

## What was there

The board rows (`appendFixture`, `sportRow`) had a star; started rows drawn
without their full object (`pinnedRow`) left an empty `span.pin-gap`; Bets legs,
substitutes, the Card's "more picks", the slip, the Edge picks and Scale's rows
had none. Each Bets leg was drawn twice: a summary line (`.og-l`: time, pick,
teams, sport, price source, opposition price, Google predictions, price, read
time, chance) and then its full board row (`.og-fx`, filled by `ogFill`). The
chips came in two orders (football: Edge, price, context, Bookies; the other
sports: price, context, tennis and college marks, Bookies). Scale's rows had
their own time and name type.

## One star

All in `index.html`, after `starIdOf`:

- `starEach(fn)`: every pick Starred can draw, in the order Starred takes them
  (the list's buckets, football, the other sports and tennis, `daylist.js`
  `played`). `renderStarred` now reads it.
- `starPool()`: the bookmark ids from that walk, and an index by
  `sport|home|away` (lower case). Cached for a minute.
- `starFor(sport, home, away, when)`: the id of that pair on the nearest day,
  or null. A Bets leg, a slip leg, an Edge pick and a Scale row find their star
  this way, so it is the same id as the pick's board row.
- `starHtml(id, label)`: the button (`data-star`, `data-label`), or the old
  `pin-gap` spacer when the id is null: a pick Starred cannot draw (a list day
  older than yesterday, an old Scale day, a leg on no board) gets no star rather
  than a bookmark that would not show.
- one delegated click handler toggles a `data-star` button, saves, calls
  `starSync(id)` (every copy of that star on the page) and redraws Starred and
  the Daily List when open (Starred lists stars; the list's Most nailed on reads
  them), keeping the scroll and the focus. `appendFixture` and `sportRow` keep
  their own handlers (`data-own`) and call `starSync` too.
- `pinnedRow(g, i, date)` takes the day so its star is the id Starred uses (it
  tries the day given, `g.date` and `g.day`, and keeps the one in the pool).

The store is unchanged: `almanac-starred`, `{date, ids}`, emptied each day.

## Bets legs drawn once

`ogCard` writes, per leg, the summary `.og-l` (now with a star), the
placeholder `.og-fx` and a hidden facts line `.og-lx` (`legFacts`). `ogFill`
draws the board row as before, then `ogLegOnce(ph)`: when a row drew, the
summary is hidden and the facts line shown, less what the row already says:
the opposition price when the row has a Bookies line (`.mkt`), the chance when
it equals the row's number, the pick when the row marks it (`.club.pick`), and
the row's price chip when the facts line gives the same price (Avg, or Hill on
Card and paper groups). The facts line takes the row's alternate band. A leg
with no row keeps its summary, which has every fact. Substitutes (`ogBench`) and
the Card's "more picks" (`cardPicksHtml`) stay one-line summaries with a star
(`.og-l.st`); the slip's and Edge's compact lines (`.tb-row.st`) too.

Found on the way: the Daily 2x also matched the "groups from the old rules"
fold (`other` in `renderToday`), so it was drawn twice; it is left out now.

## One chip order

`rowChips(g, sport)`: price (`hillChip`, which falls back to `priceChip`),
`bookGapChip`, `cxChip`, then the sport's marks: `edgeFlag` (football board
fixtures only), Celtic's Law `flagged`, `tennisWary`, `sideWaryChip` (which
carries `collegeTierChip` and `trailWaryChip`). Reserve is still added last by
the reserve fold. Used by `appendFixture` (not on Both to score, which keeps
only its Celtic flag), `sportRow` and `pinnedRow`. `cautionMark` stays on the
competition name, where every football row already has it; moving it into the
chips would have been a new place for an old mark.

## Scale rows

`scRow` gains the star column and the board's time cell (`.sc-t`: mono time
over the competition in 10px small capitals with the sport's emoji, as `.ko`)
and name type (`.club` for the pick, `.sc-v` for "v Opponent"). Scale's own
columns stay. `.ko` itself is not reused: its phone rule sets a grid area that
Scale's grid does not have. On a phone the star spans the row's left edge.

## Checked

Unit suite and nametest; `check.js` at 1280 and 400; `scratchpad/r5-row.js`
(both widths: legs drawn once, each fact once, star a leg and see it on the
Daily List, Basketball and Starred, a Scale star, a started row's star, unstar
from Starred, chip order on every football, tennis, NBA and list row) and
`r5-slip.js`; `r3-click.js` and `r4-nav.js` still pass. Local data had only
Daily 2x groups, so the Card, its substitutes and "more picks" were checked by
reading the code, not on screen.
