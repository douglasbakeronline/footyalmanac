# The Card 2x of two picks (`m7legs2`, 12 Nov 2026)

**Why.** The live Card (CARD_RULE 11, the 2x alone, 3-5 picks, 2.0-3.0
combined) built nothing for 11 or 12 Nov. On 11 Nov the funnel had five NBA
picks clear the full rule at 1.53-1.64 (Knicks, Nuggets, Pelicans, Magic,
Rockets) and none of the 22:15 and later builds made a group: any three pay
3.6x or more, over the 2x band, and a group needs three. The funnel's one
11 Nov group leans on Manacor (16:30 UK), already started by the time rule 11
shipped at 22:27, so live could not have built it. No bug: the shape of the
rule. Two of those picks pay 2.3-2.7, inside the band.

**Pre-registered** (before any result was read): `m7legs2` in
`tools/card_replay.py`, the live Card with `CARD_LEGS` (2, 3, 4, 5); the
same on five football-data seasons in `tools/card_legs_history.py`
(`cardM7.m7legs2`). Live is unchanged (3-5).

**Pool, 30 Oct to 15 Nov (`--since 2026-10-30`, pool legs only):**

| read | live groups, won, return | m7legs2 groups, won, return | m7legs2 legs | paired v live | p(worse) |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| first real price | 7, 3, -5% | 17, 8, -2% | 34 | +0.01 units a day | 0.47 |
| `--recover` | 16, 10, +51% | 25, 13, +12% | 51 | -1.14 a day | 0.80 |
| `--closing` | 6, 3, +6% | 17, 7, -11% | 34 | -0.63 a day | 0.63 |

Two-pick groups more than double the volume and land under their own
expectation (13 of 25 v 52% on `--recover`, 7 of 17 v 48% at the closing
price); their legs are longer (quoted 63-65% v live's 70%). The extra groups
are the weak ones.

**Football-data, football only (card-legs-history.yml on #266, the fit and
check seasons):**

| season | live groups, won (expected), return | m7legs2 groups, won (expected), return | m7legs2 p(loss), losing months |
|---|---|---|---|
| 2025-26 | 44, 22 (17.9), +22% | 114, 43 (53.3), -18% | 0.98, 8 of 10 |
| 2026-27 so far | 1, 0 (0.35), -100% | 8, 5 (3.4), +56% | 0.14, 0 of 3 |

On the full season the two-pick groups land ten under their expectation and
turn a +22% Card into -18%. The 2026-27 eight groups are too few to set
against that. The older seasons were not run: one full season this clear is
enough to refuse it.

**Decision.** Fails the gate: worse on the 2025-26 season by 40 points of
return and under live on two of three pool reads. No rule change; the 3-pick
minimum stays. The nightly review keeps replaying
`m7legs2`; it is reconsidered only if it beats live at p(worse) 0.30 or under
on 100+ legs on the pool and on both football-data seasons. An empty day stays
empty, which is the rule Douglas set on 11 Nov ("the groups must win at all
costs").
