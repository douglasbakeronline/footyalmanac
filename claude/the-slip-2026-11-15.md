# The slip, 15 November 2026

Douglas, 03:06 on 15 Nov, after two bets that night: a five-fold at 6.6 (one
leg short) and an eleven-fold at 3.0 (two short). "It's becoming really
pointless to be having games that are like 1.10, because you just need so
many of them to work ... make some suggestions of how we can implement a
better way of reading and automating picks." Then: "Action that."

## What the ledger says about the two slips

`results-ledger.json.gz`, 1,248 priced picks at 62%+, landed against the
price they were available at (William Hill's where it had one):

| price band | picks | landed | implied | return a leg |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1.00-1.15 | 356 | 94.7% | 95.1% | -0.4% |
| 1.15-1.30 | 297 | 80.5% | 81.9% | -1.8% |
| 1.30-1.50 | 291 | 70.1% | 72.4% | -3.3% |
| 1.50-1.80 | 213 | 53.5% | 62.0% | -13.8% |
| 1.80-2.50 | 78 | 51.3% | 50.5% | +2.4% |

By the model against the price's implied chance: model under, 833 picks,
83% landed, -2.0%; 0-5 above, -6.7%; 5-10 above, 56% landed, -13.5%; 10+
above, 49%, -8.6%. Picks at 1.30+ where the model is above the implied
chance: 361, 56% landed, -8.8%.

So the eleven-fold at about 1.105 a leg sat in the one band that holds its
value (0.996 a leg, an eleven-fold returns about 96p in the pound on
average); the five-fold at about 1.46 a leg sat in a band returning 0.967
(a five-fold about 85p). The 1.10 games are not the problem. An accumulator
cannot make an edge its legs do not have; it compounds the margin, faster
the longer the price, and the model's disagreements with the market are
where it loses.

## What was built: `slip.py`

One slip a UK day, on the Bets tab under the Card, built for expected
return rather than confidence:

- Candidates: every priced pick still to start on the day, every sport, at
  `CONF_MIN` 62%+, from the ledger (`ledger.py` runs first in deploy.yml).
- A leg's chance is the landed rate its sport and price band have shown on
  the ledger (`RATES_MIN_N` 50 graded picks, else the pooled band), never the
  model's number alone. A tally, not a fit (Rule 1 in spirit: the ledger is
  the scoreboard, and the slip only reads which bands hold their price; it
  tunes nothing in a model).
- A leg is refused when its band returns worse than `LEG_RET_MIN` (-2%, the
  margin), when the model sits more than `ABOVE_MAX` (5 points) above the
  price's implied chance, or when it is an away college pick
  (`sports.WARY_SPORTS`).
- From the strongest `CAND_MAX` (30) candidates by chance x price, the set of
  `LEGS` 3 to 5 legs from different fixtures (at most `COMP_MAX` 2 a
  competition) whose combined price sits in `TARGET` 3.0-6.0 with the highest
  expected return; offered only when that return is at least the stake
  (`EV_MIN` 1.0). Otherwise the day says why: few, short, long, spread or
  value.
- Archived to `slip-archive/<day>.json`, replaced until its first leg starts
  and frozen from then; graded from the ledger's results (a void leg struck
  from the price); `slip.json` / `slip.js` for the page.
- Pre-registered gate: `GATE_N` 100 settled slips, return positive at the
  archived price, day-resampled p(loss) <= `GATE_P` 0.10. Until then it is an
  experiment with a visible expected return, not a method.
- A walk-forward replay over the ledger's past days (each day's slip from
  that day's priced picks with rates read only from picks settled before it)
  prints with the record: at 15 Nov, 6 days could be built (the rates were
  thin before November), 2 of 6 won, +7.3%, p(loss) 0.33. Not evidence yet.

First build, 15 Nov: five legs at 3.33 (Pacific, San Diego State, Kagoshima
United, Ancona, Ujpest II), tested chance 36%, expected return 118p in the
pound. 16 Nov: no slip, the best set returns under the stake.

## The page

`slipSection` on the Bets tab, between the Card and the Edge picks: the legs
with their tested basis, model number and band return; the fold's price,
tested chance and expected return in pence per pound; the rule in one
paragraph; the site record against the gate and the replay line; and a fold
with the landed-against-the-price table by sport and band, updated every
build.

## Not done

- No other-sport Edge flag, no change to the Card, the Daily List or any
  model. The slip reads the ledger only.
- The band rates are the whole record's; a by-season or by-month split waits
  for volume.
- The 3x-6x band and the 3-5 legs are Douglas's brief; `TARGET` and `LEGS`
  are the constants to move.
