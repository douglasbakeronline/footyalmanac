# Weekend losses: which betting mistakes the record proves (23 Nov 2026)

Douglas's nine slips of 21-22 Nov all lost (eight at £100, one at £20), most by
one leg. Each claimed mistake checked on the site's own record.

Data: football archives 23 Aug to 22 Nov 2026 (13,714 graded picks, 6,161 with
a bookie price); `sports-graded.json` (1,918 graded US-sport and rugby picks).

## Proven
- Stay on the Daily List. Football: list 83.8% (185), reserve 67.3% (620),
  below the bar 55-70% 62.1% (2,130). Sports: list 91.4% (430), off-list 75%+
  79.0%, off-list 65-75% 68.9%.
- Football legs at 1.40+ are the weak links, and draws are why. Won / drawn by
  best price: 1.00-1.20 88.5 / 9.5; 1.20-1.30 81.0 / 10.7; 1.30-1.40 78.1 /
  12.8; 1.40-1.50 68.5 / 17.7; 1.50-1.70 59.2 / 24.5. The prices track the real
  rate closely.
- Over 2.5 at a 50-55% model rating landed 56% (4,828); at 1.36 it loses.
- College basketball at 70%+: home 87% (599), neutral 62% (32), away 46% (24).
  College football: home 80%, away 71%.

## Partly proven
- Leg count. Slips of each day's strongest 75%+ picks: 3-6 legs won 71-74%,
  7 legs 67%, 8 legs 62%, 10 legs 55% (33-50 days each). Leg quality matters
  more than leg count.

## Not proven
- Football away picks are not worse: 75%+ away 90.9% (33) v home 83.1%.
- Same fixture on several slips, late top-up slips, stakes: the site does not
  record Douglas's own bets.
