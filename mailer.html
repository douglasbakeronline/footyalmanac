What a daily email holds

  1. The Top 10, strongest first: kick-off, form, the model's chance and the best UK price.
  2. A suggested triple: the three most likely picks paying ×1.30 or more together.
  3. The Scale bet, if you've chosen it: the same winners with a second condition, for bigger odds.
  4. The last three days of the Daily List, every pick and how it went.

Honest numbers

Short prices land often and pay little, and a high hit rate is not the same as a profit. Every pick is graded the morning after on How it went, losses included.
